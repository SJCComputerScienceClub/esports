THE SQUAD

About Us.

The SJC eSports Club brings together students who love gaming, competition, and community. Whether you're a casual player or a competitive contender, there's a place for you here.

Our club creates opportunities to play together, build friendships, develop strategies, and represent St. John's College High School with pride.

You don't have to be the best player to belong. Teamwork, sportsmanship, school spirit, and having fun are all part of the game.
